# SQL Music Store Data Analysis

## Project Overview
Analyzed a Music Store database using PostgreSQL to gain insights into customer purchases, sales performance, invoice trends, popular tracks, and artist revenue.

## Tools Used
- PostgreSQL

## SQL Concepts Used
- SELECT, WHERE, ORDER BY, LIMIT
- GROUP BY and HAVING
- Aggregate Functions (COUNT, SUM, AVG)
- INNER JOIN
- Subqueries and Nested Subqueries
- DISTINCT
- Date Functions (EXTRACT)

## Business Questions Answered
1. Who is the senior-most employee based on job level?
2. Which countries have the most invoices?
3. What are the top 3 invoice values?
4. Which customers have made more than 5 purchases?
5. How many customers are there in each country?
6. Find all invoices generated during 2017.
7. Which customers spent more than the average customer spending?
8. What are the top 5 best-selling tracks?
9. Which city generated the highest revenue?
10. How many invoices were generated in each month?
11. What is the total revenue generated by each artist?
12. Who is the best customer based on total spending?
13. Who are the customers listening to Rock music?
14. Which 10 artists have the most Rock tracks?
15. Which tracks are longer than the average track length?
